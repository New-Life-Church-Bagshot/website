Good to know

Ask us anything ↗
When and where will you meet?

Our first Sunday is {{sundays.launchLabel}} at {{contact.street}}, {{contact.town}}, {{contact.postcode}}. We’ll meet every Sunday at {{sundays.serviceStarts}}, with doors open for coffee from {{sundays.doorsOpen}}.

What should I wear?

Wear whatever you feel comfortable in.

Do I need to book or know what to do?

Just come along. You can take a seat, listen and join in as much as you like. You’re welcome whatever you believe.

Can I bring my children?

Yes. Children are welcome to stay with you. We’ll share what will be available for different ages before our Sunday gatherings start.

Where can I park?

There’s a small car park outside the church, but it fills quickly. There’s also parking by the Co-op next door and on nearby roads. Check the signs for any restrictions. See parking charges at The Square.

Who can I ask about access?

Email us to talk through what you need before you visit.
